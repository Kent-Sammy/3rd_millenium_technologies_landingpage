# CLAUDE.md — Corporate AI Training & Transformation Website

> **Purpose:** Project brief for Claude Code (or another Claude-powered coding environment) to build a polished, responsive website for a Nigerian B2B company that helps organisations train employees to use AI, create AI-powered content, and implement practical AI workflows.
>
> **Important:** The company name, brand identity, final prices, contact details and proof points are not yet confirmed. Use clearly labelled placeholders and make these values easy to update. Do not invent client logos, testimonials, case studies, certifications, results or contact details.

## 1. Product vision

Build a credible, modern corporate website for an AI enablement company serving businesses in Nigeria, with potential to expand across Africa.

The company helps organisations:
1. Train employees to use AI in the context of their actual jobs.
2. Use AI for creative work, including video production, presentations, pitch decks, imagery and content.
3. Improve work processes with AI workflows, automation, integrations and agents.
4. Choose AI tools and subscriptions in a cost-conscious way.
5. Adopt AI responsibly, with attention to privacy, security, permissions and human review.
6. Measure learning, adoption and business outcomes.

This is **not simply an online course marketplace** and should not look like a generic AI startup landing page. It is a B2B training, enablement, advisory and implementation service.

**Core positioning:** We help organisations turn AI into a practical workplace capability.

**Suggested hero headline:** Your team is capable of more. Give them the AI skills to prove it.

**Supporting copy:** We help organisations train their people, improve everyday workflows and put AI to work across the business. From finance and HR to marketing, creative production and software development, we turn AI from something employees experiment with into something they can use confidently in their work.

Primary CTA: **Book a Consultation**  
Secondary CTA: **Explore Our Programmes**

## 5. Visual and brand direction

The website should feel professional, credible to senior corporate buyers, modern, practical, human and premium without being inaccessible.

Avoid:
- Generic AI website clichés
- Excessive neon gradients and glowing orbs
- Robot illustrations as the main visual language
- Huge walls of text
- Fake statistics, client logos or testimonials
- Unsupported promises such as “10x productivity” or “guaranteed ROI”
- Too many animations
- Overuse of glassmorphism
- Lorem ipsum

Prefer:
- Strong typographic hierarchy
- Efficient whitespace
- Clear contrast and readable text
- Subtle motion only where useful
- Appropriate workplace photography or practical visual examples
- Programme cards and business-outcome callouts
- Consistent iconography
- Clear CTA hierarchy
- Responsive cards and tables

Do not lock final brand colours until references or brand decisions are supplied. If none are supplied, choose a restrained corporate palette and document it in design tokens.

## 8. Pricing approach

The owner has proposed pricing based on participant count and monthly, three-month and six-month engagements.

The public website should initially explain pricing factors and encourage visitors to request a tailored quote. Do not publish indicative per-person prices as final until the owner confirms them.

Suggested copy:

### Pricing that fits your team and objectives

Every organisation starts from a different place. Our proposal considers the number of participants, programme selected, engagement duration, delivery format and any specialist implementation support required.

- **Monthly programme:** A focused engagement for an immediate skills gap or a specific team.
- **Three-month programme:** A structured learning journey with time for practice, application and review.
- **Six-month programme:** An extended programme for multiple teams or deeper capability development.
- **Custom enterprise programme:** For large teams, multiple departments, specialist technical training or AI implementation.

CTA: **Request a Tailored Proposal**

Pricing rules:
- Training and implementation must be priced separately unless expressly bundled.
- Third-party software subscriptions are separate unless explicitly included.
- In-person delivery, travel and venue costs may be quoted separately.
- Clarify cohort size, sessions, support and deliverables in proposals.
- Do not promise unlimited support or unscoped outcomes.
- Keep prices easy to update.

If a pricing calculator is implemented, label it as an estimate and explain that the formal proposal confirms scope and price.

## 11. Technical implementation instructions

First inspect the repository and existing project structure. Do not replace a working project or introduce a new framework without a reason.

If starting from an empty repository:
1. Choose a stable stack compatible with the environment.
2. Prefer a component-based framework supported by the host.
3. Use TypeScript if supported by the setup.
4. Use maintainable styling and reusable design tokens.
5. Keep programme content in structured data.
6. Keep form integration behind a server-side or secure service boundary.
7. Document setup, development, build and deployment steps.

Do not assume a hosting platform, backend, CRM, form provider, analytics tool or payment system has been selected.

Before coding:
- Inspect files, framework and existing assets.
- Identify constraints.
- Briefly summarise the implementation plan.
- Build the working experience, not just a static mockup.

After coding:
- Run available lint, type-check and build commands.
- Fix errors introduced by the implementation.
- Check routes and form states.
- Verify mobile layouts.
- Report anything incomplete.
